Privacy

The Outis demo plays video. Its analytics and crash reports run only after you agree, and you can change your mind at any time.

This covers the web demo and the Android and iOS sample apps built from the same code. The Outis library itself collects nothing; only the demo uses analytics.

What is collected, and when

ChoiceWhatWho receives it
Essential
always on		 Nothing about you leaves your device. The diagnostics log and your privacy choice are stored on the device. Fetching the stream list and the videos reveals your IP address to their hosts, as any web request does. GitHub Pages and the video hosts, for the requests themselves
Ad examples
when you play one		 The web demo loads Google's ad SDK on every visit, which reveals your IP address to Google. Playing a video with client-side ads asks Google's ad servers for the ads, and they may set cookies of their own. Google (privacy)
Performance
only if you agree		 How well each video plays: startup time, stalls, quality and errors, with the video, your device and browser type, an approximate location and a random viewer id kept on your device. In the apps, also crash reports: what failed, the device model and system version, and an installation id. Mux (privacy); for crash reports, Google Firebase Crashlytics (privacy)
Marketing
only if you agree		 Which videos are chosen, started, finished or fail, and how long a visit lasts, linked by a random identifier: a cookie (_ga) on the web, an app-instance id in the apps. Never the address of a stream you type in yourself. Google Analytics (privacy)

Until you choose, nothing in the last two rows is collected.

Changing your choice

Open Player settings → Privacy & data in the demo. A change takes effect at once, except that the apps' crash reporting follows it from their next launch. Reports from a session you have not agreed to are deleted, not sent.
